# Reviewer guide – Loan Intake

You only need to act when a case reaches you. The system handles everything else.

## When you get a new case
You'll get a notification in **Teams** (Approvals app) and an email in **Outlook** titled *"Loan application: [name]"*.

## Reviewing a case
1. Open the approval in Teams → **Approvals**, or from the email.
2. Check the **DSR** shown on the card. If the case was flagged, its DSR is above 60%.
3. Click **Open in list** to see the full application: income, commitments and documents.

## Making a decision
1. Type a short reason in **Comments**, for example *"Approved – DSR within limit"* or *"Rejected – DSR 75% over limit"*.
2. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

That's it. The status, your comment, the client email and the team update all happen automatically.

## Finding cases
Open the **Loan Applications** list. The **Status** column tells you where each case is:

| Status | Meaning | Do you need to act? |
|---|---|---|
| Submitted | Waiting for your decision | Yes |
| Flagged | Waiting for your decision, DSR above 60% | Yes, review carefully |
| Incomplete | Missing documents; client already emailed | No |
| Approved / Rejected | Decided | No |

## Good to know
- Always add a comment. It's saved as the record of why you decided.
- Cases marked *Incomplete* never come to you; the client has to resubmit.
- If an approval card doesn't appear, check the Approvals app in Teams under **Received**.
